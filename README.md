# Team Fortress 2 Takeover

**Terraria** × Team Fortress 2 · visual · idea by the team

Bring Team Fortress 2 into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I dropped Team Fortress 2 into Terraria: BLU mercs, rocket jumps, a flamethrower and a Tank. Poor Jeff the Guide never stood a chance.

## Install

Copy Sigf.tmod into Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Mods, then enable "SIGF Mix" in tModLoader's Workshop > Manage Mods menu and start a world. Monsters become BLU mercs and the Tank boss rolls in.
